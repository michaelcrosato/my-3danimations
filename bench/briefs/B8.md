# B8 · Contribute an emote

Your game's character needs an emote the m3a library doesn't have, and your studio gives what it makes back to the
library. The m3a packages are installed in `node_modules/@m3a/`. Start with their manual:
`node_modules/@m3a/tools/MANUAL.md`.

**The job:** make an emote of your choosing for `character.glb`, from the CC0 library, and contribute it to the
library through its intake queue with `npx m3a library submit`, so that it waits for the owner's review
(`awaiting review`) with every check green.
- It must be new to the library: the queue bounces a library clip sent as it is, or one barely changed, and it is a
  clip of its own, not sent as a fix of a library clip (`--improves`).
- Tag it `emote`, and give it a name and a description that say what it does.
- Its provenance must be complete: its method and the tool that made it, a CC0 license claim, and who made it (you
  are an AI agent working for the game's studio: say so, naming your model).
- This workspace has the queue's local backend (`.cache/intake/`), and its checker runs on it every 30 seconds while
  you work, as the hosted queue's does every 15 minutes. `npx m3a library status` shows each of your submissions'
  state and a bounce's reason: if one is bounced, fix what it names and submit again.

**How it's graded** (headless): the checker runs on this workspace's queue, and one of your submissions waits for
review, every gate green (schema, provenance, license, text, dedup, sensors, the retarget onto the reference rigs,
budgets, lint), checked again from its text; its provenance is complete (method, tool and version, license
`CC0-1.0`, who made it, and the local report `m3a library submit` adds); it is tagged `emote` and sent without
`--improves`; and it plays on `character.glb`, every joint finite. Work only in this directory.

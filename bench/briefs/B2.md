# B2 · Attack with hit timing

You're wiring a game character's attack. The project in this directory has the character (`character.glb`, a skinned
humanoid in metres, facing +Z) and the m3a packages installed in `node_modules/@m3a/`. Start with their manual:
`node_modules/@m3a/tools/MANUAL.md`.

**The job:** implement `createFighter` in `src/fighter.ts` (its types are there). The game calls
`fighter.update(dt, { attack })` each frame (60 a second), with `attack` true on the frame the attack button is
pressed:
- standing, the character idles (a clip from the CC0 library);
- pressed, it throws a punch (another), then goes back to idling;
- the game's `damage(10)` is called once per punch, on the frame the punch lands: when the punching hand moves fastest
  toward its target (the clip's measured strike: an event in the clip at that time is the usual way), not when the
  button is pressed or when the punch ends.

`npm run check` drives your fighter as the grader will (two presses, two seconds apart) and prints when it called
`damage`, and for each punch its strike and the frames damage may come on.

**How it's graded** (headless, from the character's bones): before the first press and after the last punch it idles
(the body moves: a pose held still is no idle); after each press the character punches (a hand reaches forward and
strikes faster than one hip height a second against the pelvis), and within 1.5 s goes back to idling (the hand comes
at least half way back); `damage(10)` is called exactly once per punch, never without one, from two frames before its
strike to the frame the hand is farthest out (the strike: the last time the punching hand's forward speed peaks faster
than one hip height a second before it is farthest out, the punch's own peak, not a fade into a stance before it;
`strike.ts` finds it, for the grader and the check alike); no transform is ever NaN. Work only in this directory.

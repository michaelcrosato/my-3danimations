# B3 · Reactions

You're wiring a game character's hit reactions. The project in this directory has the character (`character.glb`, a
skinned humanoid in metres, facing +Z) and the m3a packages installed in `node_modules/@m3a/`. Start with their manual:
`node_modules/@m3a/tools/MANUAL.md`.

**The job:** implement `createReactor` in `src/reactor.ts` (its types are there). The game calls
`reactor.update(dt, { speed, hit })` each frame (60 a second): `speed` is the ground speed it asks, in the
character's hip heights a second (0 standing, 1.2 walking), and `hit` is `"light"` or `"heavy"` on the frame a hit
lands (else absent).
- Standing or walking: idle, or walk travelling at the asked speed (move `character.position` along its facing), with
  clips from the CC0 library.
- A light hit: a flinch on the upper body only, while the legs keep walking as before.
- A heavy hit: knocked down to the ground; it lies there 1 s, then gets up, and walks again.
- The feet, the hips and the head never sink through the floor.

`npm run check` drives your reactor as the grader will (walking from 1 s, a light hit at 3 s, a heavy one at 6 s) and
prints every half second how high the hips are and where the character is.

**How it's graded** (headless, from the character's bones, against the same reactor run without the light hit): walking,
the hips travel at the speed asked (1.2 hip heights a second, ±10%) and the lower foot stands still at times (slower
than the m3a sensors' standing speed); during the light hit's 0.8 s the legs (hips, knees and ankles against the pelvis)
stay within 2 cm of the walk without it, while the hands or head move at least 5 cm from it; within 1.5 s of the heavy
hit the hips go below 45% of their standing height, lie on the ground (under 15%) for 1.3 to 3.5 s (the 1 s it lies,
the fall and the start of the rise), then are back above 85% within 3 s; the character walks again by the end; the feet,
pelvis and head never go more than 5% of the hip height under the floor; no transform is ever NaN. Work only in this
directory.

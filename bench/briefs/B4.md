# B4 · Retarget onto a VRM avatar

You're putting a sneak on a game avatar. The project in this directory has the avatar (`character.glb`: a skinned
humanoid in metres, facing +Z, its bones named as a VRM 1.0 avatar's humanoid, with proportions unlike the people the
library was captured from) and the m3a packages installed in `node_modules/@m3a/`. Start with their manual:
`node_modules/@m3a/tools/MANUAL.md`.

**The job:** implement `createSneaker` in `src/sneak.ts` (its types are there). Each frame the game calls
`sneaker.update(dt)`: the avatar sneaks forward with a crouched sneaking walk loop from the CC0 library, travelling the
way its feet push it (move `character.position` by the root motion), its feet still on the ground while they stand.
Check it with the m3a sensors before you're done.

`npm run check` drives your sneaker as the grader will (6 s) and prints how low the hips go and how far the avatar
travels; measure its feet with the sensors as the manual's "Check" shows.

**How it's graded** (headless, from the avatar's bones): it sneaks (the hips at most 85% of their standing height on
average after the first second, travelling forward at least 15% of the hip height a second); the lower foot stands
still at times (slower than the sensors' standing speed) and, while a foot stands, the m3a sensors' slide and skate stay
under their limits; nothing the sensors read of its bones fails but what a capture's
bones would (jitter and pops of the joints the bones don't carry are filled in); no transform is ever NaN. Work only in
this directory.
